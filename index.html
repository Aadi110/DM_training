Our philosophy

More than a product. A piece of Nepal.

Himalayan Heritage is imagined as a marketplace where traditional Nepali craftsmanship meets modern shopping. Every collection celebrates the makers, materials and stories behind Nepal's cultural products.

From handwoven textiles and lokta paper to Himalayan tea and artisan decor, our goal is to make heritage easier to discover while keeping the focus on craftsmanship.
